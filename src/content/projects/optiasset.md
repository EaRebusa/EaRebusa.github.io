- University capstone research project awarded a 1.0 grade mark and 2nd Place for Best in Research.
- Fleet-wide hardware diagnostics executed asynchronously through FastAPI BackgroundTasks with dedicated session lifecycles and rollback mechanisms.
- Integrated scikit-learn models to compute asset degradation scores against manufacturer hardware baselines.
- Comprehensive reporting engine producing automated CSV audit summaries and Matplotlib/FPDF2 generated PDF hardware health reports.
